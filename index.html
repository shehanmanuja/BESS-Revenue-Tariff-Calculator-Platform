The Opportunity

Underutilized Solar is Wasted Revenue

Commercial solar plants without storage sell excess generation during low-demand daytime hours for modest rates, leaving massive peak-period value uncaptured.

Low Daytime Feed-In

Standard daytime export contracts pay only ~22.00 LKR/kWh, while grid peak power costs exceed 45+ LKR/kWh.

Midday Grid Curtailment

The BESS Solution

Understand Your BESS Tariff Calculation

The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) and Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) have established formal tariff structures for prosumers integrating battery storage into existing solar plants.

CRITICAL REGULATORY RULE: The prosumer formulas shown below strictly apply to eligible Rooftop Solar PV installations (≥ 250 kW) interconnected under Net Plus / Net Accounting agreements. Ground-mounted solar power plants operate under separate utility-scale avoided cost / PPA frameworks and cannot apply the prosumer rooftop formula.

Enterprise Technology

Why Commercial Solar Operators Choose BESS

Modern utility-grade Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) containers offer superior round-trip efficiency, intrinsic fire safety, and unmatched revenue enhancement for existing solar assets.

Peak Tariff Arbitrage

Shift low-value daytime solar generation (22.00 LKR/kWh) into the high-value 18:30 – 22:30 priority feed-in tariff (up to 47.67 LKR/kWh), capturing an immediate +116% revenue spread per discharged unit.

Eliminate Curtailment Loss

When utility grid substations order curtailment or reverse-power limitations during low midday feeder loads, the BESS absorbs 100% of generated solar, preventing financial loss and wasted kilowatt-hours.

Tier-1 LFP Chemistry & Safety

Utilizes Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) chemistry with non-propagating thermal runaways, integrated aerosol fire suppression, and liquid HVAC thermal control providing 6,000+ cycle operational durability.
